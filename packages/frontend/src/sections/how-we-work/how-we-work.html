How we work

Four stages, fixed price at stage two

You will know the cost and the delivery date before any code is written, and you own the source from day one.

  1. Stage 1 · free

    Walk the process with us

    A 45-minute call, or a visit if you are nearby. We watch the current process, count the manual steps and find where the time actually goes.

  2. Stage 2 · 2–4 days

    Screens and a fixed quote

    You get clickable screens, a written scope and one price. If your sketch came from the design studio, this stage starts from your layout.

  3. Stage 3 · 2–10 weeks

    Build in weekly releases

    Something usable lands every week on a staging link. You test with real data and real staff, and we adjust while it is still cheap to adjust.

  4. Stage 4 · ongoing

    Hand over and support

    Source code, documentation and a training session for your team. Support and small changes continue monthly only if you want them.
